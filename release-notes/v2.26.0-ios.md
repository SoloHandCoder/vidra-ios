## New
- **Downloads on iPhone:** save movies and episodes, pause and resume, Wi-Fi only if you like. Downloads play in the iPhone player with subtitles and pick up where you left off. (Downloads run while Vidra is open.)
- **Skip intro:** for anime the intro times come from AniSkip. For other shows Vidra learns them: skip the same stretch near the start of two episodes, and the next ones show a **Skip intro** (or **Skip recap**) button. The auto-skip setting skips by itself.
- **Spoiler shield:** tap **Hide spoilers** above the episode list to blur pictures, titles and descriptions of episodes you haven't watched yet.
- **Tappable party invites:** Invite now shares a normal web link that works in any chat app. It opens Vidra straight into the party, or shows where to get Vidra.
- **Pin to Home:** show your watchlist or any of your lists as rows on Home (Settings → Home Page Layout, the 📌 rows). There's a **Customize Home** button at the bottom of Home.

Install the `.ipa` the same way as before (AltStore, SideStore or Sideloadly); your data is kept.
