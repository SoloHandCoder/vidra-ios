## New
- **Themes:** Midnight, Nord, Dracula, Forest, Rosé, Sepia and **Auto** (follows your device's light or dark mode), six more accent colours, and a picker for any accent colour you like.
- **Watch party polls:** tap 📊 in the chat to ask a question with 2–6 options, or fill them from the queue. Everyone votes with a tap; the host can play the winner straight away.
- **Up next** now marks the episode you just finished as watched when it moves you on.

## Faster
- The app starts with about a third less code to load; pages load when you open them.
- Much less work while downloading, while a video plays, and on the Home screen.

## Fixes
- Watch party: others see you leave right away, a slow reconnect no longer ends the party, ready checks can't get stuck, and a party without a host picks one.

Install the `.ipa` the same way as before (AltStore, SideStore or Sideloadly); your data is kept.
