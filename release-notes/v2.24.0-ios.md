## Streambert is now Vidra

- **New name and icon:** the app is called Vidra, with a new icon. It installs as an update over Streambert, and your profiles, history, downloads and settings are kept.
- **New home:** the project moved to `SoloHandCoder/vidra-android`, `vidra-desktop` and `vidra-ios`. Old links redirect.
- **Invite links** for the watch party are now `vidra://party/CODE`. Old `streambert://` links still open the app.

Vidra is based on [Streambert](https://github.com/truelockmc/streambert) by truelockmc.

Install the `.ipa` the same way as before (AltStore, SideStore or Sideloadly); your data is kept.
