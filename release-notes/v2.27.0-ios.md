## New
- **Downloads keep going in the background:** leave Vidra or lock your iPhone and downloads continue. You get a notification when one finishes.
- **Up next into the next season:** when you finish a season's last episode, up next offers episode 1 of the next season.
- **Continue on another device:** start something on one device and open Vidra on another. A card offers **Continue here** at the same second (needs cloud sync).
- **Achievements:** 15 badges in your Library, like Binge watcher, Marathon, Night owl, On fire and Film buff, with a pop-up when you earn one. They follow you across your devices.

Install the `.ipa` the same way as before (AltStore, SideStore or Sideloadly); your data is kept.
